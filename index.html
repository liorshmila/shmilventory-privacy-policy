# Privacy Policy — Shmilventory **Last updated: November 2, 2026** ## 1. Introduction Shmilventory is a business inventory management application developed by Lior Shmila. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when individuals use Shmilventory. The application is intended for authorized personnel of organizations that use it to manage inventory, products, employees, branches, and related business operations. ## 2. Information We Process Shmilventory processes information necessary to provide its inventory management services, which may include: - **Account and employee information:** Names, phone numbers, employee identifiers, authentication identifiers, account status, organizational assignments, and access permissions. - **Inventory information:** Product barcodes, stock quantities, inventory scans, inventory sessions, branch information, timestamps, and related operational records. - **Product information:** Product names, barcodes, SKUs, supplier information, product photographs, and product-management records. - **Activity and audit information:** Records of actions performed within the application, including associated users, dates, times, and affected business records. - **Technical and authentication information:** Information required to authenticate users, maintain sessions, secure access, and operate the service. Information may be provided by authorized organizational administrators, entered by users, or generated through normal application activity. ## 3. How We Use Information Information is processed to: - Authenticate users through phone-number and SMS verification. - Manage employees, organizational roles, branches, and permissions. - Record and manage inventory and product information. - Provide inventory reports, exports, and operational insights. - Maintain accountability through activity and audit records. - Protect the application and its data against unauthorized access. - Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of the service. Shmilventory does not use personal information for targeted advertising or sell personal information to advertisers. ## 4. Authentication and Access Control Shmilventory uses phone-number-based authentication with SMS verification. Authentication information is processed through Supabase Authentication and the service providers involved in delivering verification messages. Access to organizational information is managed through user roles and permissions. Authorized administrators may manage employee access within their organizational responsibilities. Signing out ends the user's authenticated application session but does not automatically delete organizational records or the user's account. ## 5. Cloud and Device Storage Shmilventory uses Supabase cloud services to support authentication, database operations, inventory management, and product-image storage. Certain information may also be stored on the user's device, including authentication session data, application preferences, locally cached or operational records, and temporary files generated for reports or sharing. Information is processed for the purposes of providing and maintaining the application's business functions. ## 6. Camera, Photographs, and Barcode Scanning Shmilventory requests camera access to scan product barcodes and capture product photographs. Users may also select product images from their device's photo library through the operating system's image-selection interface. Selected or captured product photographs may be uploaded to cloud storage and associated with product records. Camera access is not used for advertising or behavioral tracking. ## 7. Third-Party Services Shmilventory relies on service providers and software components necessary for its operation, including: - **Supabase:** Authentication, cloud database services, file storage, and related infrastructure. - **SMS delivery providers:** Delivery of authentication verification messages. - **Google Play and Android services:** Application distribution, updates, barcode-scanning components, and operating-system functionality. These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their respective services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. Shmilventory does not integrate advertising networks or application analytics SDKs for behavioral advertising. ## 8. Information Sharing and Exports Authorized users may generate and share inventory reports or export files through their device's available sharing applications. The recipient and sharing method are selected by the user. Information shared outside Shmilventory may then be handled according to the recipient service's privacy practices. Organizational information may also be accessible to authorized users and administrators according to their assigned permissions. We do not sell personal information. ## 9. Data Security Shmilventory uses authentication and role-based access controls as part of its approach to protecting organizational information. Access to application functions and business records is governed by permissions associated with users and their organizational responsibilities. Cloud infrastructure and device-level security features also contribute to protecting information. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. ## 10. Data Retention and Deletion Information is retained as needed to provide inventory management services, maintain operational and audit records, support organizational accountability, and meet applicable legal obligations. Some records may remain in historical or audit information after an employee's access is deactivated or a product is removed from active use. Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of personal information may be submitted using the contact details below. Requests will be assessed in accordance with applicable law and relevant organizational recordkeeping obligations. ## 11. Children's Privacy Shmilventory is intended for business and organizational inventory management. It is not directed to children. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances that require its removal under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address the matter. ## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application, its data-processing practices, or applicable legal requirements. The updated policy will be made available through the application's published Privacy Policy page. ## 13.
